Dear Emma,

Four years. That's how long it had been since we last stood in the same room. Four years of messages, calls, time zones and "one day, maybe".

And then, this October, we got on a plane together for the very first time and flew to Morocco for the wedding. I still smile when I think about it. I'm so glad I found the courage to ask, and so grateful that you trusted me enough to say yes.

Platzhalter: ein konkreter Lieblingsmoment aus Marokko, z. B. ein Abend, ein Lachen, ein Ort

Since then I miss you more than I can fit into a letter. A little more every day.

So this Christmas I'm counting the sleeps until I'm in Toronto and can finally hug you again. I can't wait to see you.

Merry Christmas.
